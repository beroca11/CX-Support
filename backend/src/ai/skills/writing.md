---
name: writing
when: always
---

Write like a person sending one message. Use short, plain sentences. Cut filler such as "please be advised", "it is important to note", and "as per".
Use active voice. For every action on this issue, use we: "We reviewed", "We confirmed", "We will update you". Do not use passive voice ("was reviewed", "has been confirmed") or a distant subject ("the team", "Coinbase", "it was determined") for those actions.
Leave the greeting "I am {name} from Executive Escalation Team" as it is unless the note asks to change it.
